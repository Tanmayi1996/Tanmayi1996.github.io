Common questions

Is the DocScore Lab analyzer still free?

Yes. The browser-based scoring tool remains free to use. The paid options add a manual review by a technical writer; they do not lock the free score behind a paywall.

Does the automated score guarantee documentation quality?

No. It uses simple content heuristics. A human review considers context and reader needs, but technical facts still need confirmation from the product or engineering owner.

How do I pay?

Payment is not collected on this static website. First email the requested package. After the scope is confirmed, payment instructions or a secure payment link can be shared before work begins.

Can you review confidential or internal documentation?

Discuss the document type and an approved secure transfer method first. Do not paste credentials, customer data, or sensitive internal content into the public analyzer.

Can I request a custom engagement?

Yes. For larger documentation sets, ongoing editorial support, docs-as-code improvements, or a documentation workflow review, send a short scope description by email.

Not sure which review fits?

Share the document type and approximate size. We'll confirm the best scope before you commit.
